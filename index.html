B2B lead generation agency: how we bring you enterprise clients

Plemio is a full-cycle B2B lead generation agency. We don’t sell traffic or run ads: our job is to get you into a direct conversation with decision-makers at the companies you actually want to work with. To do that, we build and verify a list for your specific niche, write a sequence of personalized emails, warm up the sending mailboxes and handle the conversation until the prospect confirms interest.

B2B lead generation differs from typical performance marketing: longer sales cycles, several decision-makers on the buyer’s side and bigger deal sizes. The “spend the budget, get inquiries” logic doesn’t work here — you need to reach the right company and the right role with the right message. That’s why we start not with sending but with the ideal customer profile: industry, revenue, region, decision-maker title and the problem your product solves. The accuracy of this profile directly determines how many emails turn into replies.

What B2B leads mean in our model

The B2B leads we deliver are not a contact export from a database. A lead is a reply in which a prospect asked for a proposal, a meeting, a call or more information. Along with the contact you get the company name, the person’s title and the full email thread, so your rep enters the conversation with context instead of starting cold. Replies like “unsubscribe me” or “not relevant” are not leads and are never billed.

Which companies B2B lead generation fits

Winning B2B clients with personal emails works best where a complex product is sold to another company and more than one person makes the decision:

  • manufacturing, wholesale and industrial equipment;
  • IT, software development and enterprise systems;
  • logistics, freight and warehousing;
  • engineering, design and construction contracting;
  • professional services: consulting, audit, legal and HR services;
  • and many other niches.

In these niches paid search is either overpriced or fails to reach the right role, while an email lands directly in the decision-maker’s inbox and lets you approach a specific company from your target list.

How pricing and timelines work

Pricing for our B2B lead generation services has two parts. The first is a fixed monthly fee: it covers the campaign infrastructure — the sending platform, contact data, domain setup and mailbox warm-up. The second is payment for leads: after each month, and only for replies with confirmed interest. Not for opens, not for clicks. First leads usually arrive on day 10–14 — that time goes into warming up the Google Workspace mailboxes and preparing the copy, without which emails land in spam. After that the volume scales: to get more leads, we add domains and mailboxes and expand the list segments without changing the pricing model.

For your sales team the result looks like this: instead of cold calling and sorting through irrelevant contacts, reps get a flow of inquiries from companies that have already replied and shown interest. That turns enterprise client acquisition into a managed process with a clear cost per inquiry rather than a series of one-off spikes.
